Royalty Recovery, Rights & Catalog Advisory

We recover what was always yours.

Unlike royalty collection tools built purely around automated audits, JUNIUS pairs that recovery work with real consultation, rights management, and catalog advisory, treating the money we find as the starting point of a lasting financial foundation, not the end of the engagement.

JUNIUS exists because the artists who built the foundation of American music, music that shaped culture across the world, were routinely underpaid, under-registered, and overlooked by an industry that profited from their work. That debt has never been settled.

JUNIUS combines royalty recovery, rights management, business administration, and catalog advisory to help artists and heirs understand what they own, what they're owed, and how to protect it for generations to come. Recovery is just the start. We make sure the knowledge stays with you, because understanding your own catalog is part of what keeps it protected. In many ways, JUNIUS isn't simply a music business company. It's an economic empowerment and legacy preservation company, dedicated to helping creators reclaim what was always theirs.
